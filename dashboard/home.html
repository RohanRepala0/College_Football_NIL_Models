Public-data analytics on what college football players are paid and what rosters deliver, built from the College Sports Commission's published pay distributions, CollegeFootballData, and four published team ratings. Refreshed by scheduled jobs; every number traces back to a public source.

About the data

  • Pay distributions are the College Sports Commission's published percentiles, captured daily and versioned. Revenue share and NIL Go are separate reports and are never combined.
  • Individual contracts are private. Roster values are program totals at the CSC's market rates, not what any school actually pays.
  • Rosters, production, scores and the four team ratings come from CollegeFootballData.com.
  • Source code and every intermediate file: github.com/RohanRepala0/College_Football_NIL_Models.
